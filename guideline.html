Community Guidelines

Welcome to the Find Ur Partner community. We want everyone to feel safe and respected. Please follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Everyone

Treat every member with kindness, respect and understanding.

2. Be Honest

Always provide correct information and do not pretend to be someone else.

3. No Bullying or Harassment

Bullying, threats, discrimination and harassment are not allowed.

4. Protect Your Privacy

Do not share your passwords, private messages or personal details without permission.

5. Report Bad Behaviour

Report suspicious accounts, inappropriate messages and harmful behaviour to the platform team.

6. Follow the Rules

Members who break these guidelines may have their accounts restricted or suspended.
