ZODIACMATE

ZODIACMATE is a mutual game created to stir the feelings and intimacy of one lover and another. The game has rules where every soulmate must follow. first, a soul is expected to search for a unique character that matches his or her feelings and traits. After completing the first task, he or she proceeds to compare the qualities to achieve the goal. lovers matches his or her birthday month and day with his or her lover's birthday month and day. if they perfectly match, they share their experience and lover story then get more intimate about themselves
