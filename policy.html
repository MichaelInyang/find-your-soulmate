privacy Policy

Welcome to Find Your soulmate. Our goal is to build a safe, respectful, and welcoming space where everyone feels comfortable making meaningful connections.

1. Introduction and Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Welcome to Find Your Partner (also referred to as "Find Your Soulmate", "we", "us", or "our"). We understand that searching for a meaningful relationship requires sharing personal details about who you are, what you value, and what you are looking for in a partner. Because of this, protecting your privacy and earning your trust are at the very center of everything we build.

This Privacy Policy explains in clear detail what personal information we collect when you visit our website or use our services, how we use and protect that information, when it may be shared, and the choices you have to control your data. By creating an account, browsing profiles, or interacting with other members on our platform, you confirm that you have read and understood the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect From You

To create a welcoming, authentic, and effective matchmaking experience, we collect several categories of information. First, we collect information you provide directly when you register and customize your profile. This includes your full name or display name, email address, password, date of birth, gender, city or general location, profile photographs, personal biography, hobbies, lifestyle details, and partner preferences.

Second, we collect information generated as you use the platform. This includes the messages you send and receive with other members, profiles you view or express interest in, search filters you apply, and any reports or customer support requests you submit to our team. Finally, we automatically collect standard technical and device information whenever you access our site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and timestamps of your visits, which help us keep the service reliable and secure.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and continuously improve Find Your Partner. Primarily, your profile details and preferences allow our system to display your profile to relevant members and recommend compatible matches based on shared interests, age range, and location. Your account credentials and contact details are also used to authenticate your logins, send important account notifications, and respond promptly when you reach out to our support team.

In addition to matchmaking, we rely on user data and technical logs to protect the safety and integrity of our community. We analyze usage patterns to detect suspicious activity, prevent spam or automated bot accounts, investigate reports of harassment or fraud, and enforce our Terms of Use. We also review aggregated, non-identifiable statistics to understand which features our members enjoy most so we can fix bugs and design better tools for building genuine connections.

4. How and When Your Information Is Shared

When you create a profile on Find Your Partner, certain details are meant to be seen by other members so you can meet and connect. Your display name, age, general location, photos, bio, and stated interests will be visible to other registered users on the platform. However, sensitive details such as your email address, password, exact home address, and private messages are never displayed publicly on your profile.

We want to be completely clear: we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertising networks. We only share limited data with trusted technical service providers—such as cloud hosting platforms, email delivery services, and security partners—who work on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements. We may also disclose information if required to do so by a valid court order, law enforcement request, or to protect the physical safety of our users and the public.

5. Data Security and Storage Practices

Safeguarding your personal information against unauthorized access, accidental loss, theft, or misuse is a responsibility we take seriously. We implement industry-standard security measures across our platform, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL), secure password hashing so plain-text passwords are never stored, restricted administrative access, and regular system monitoring for potential vulnerabilities.

While we work hard to defend your data using strong technical and organizational safeguards, no website or internet transmission can ever be guaranteed to be 100% immune from every cyber threat. We encourage all members to do their part by choosing a strong, unique password, keeping their login details private, and logging out when using shared or public devices.

6. Cookies and Local Browser Storage

Find Your Partner uses cookies, session tokens, and similar browser storage technologies to give you a smooth and personalized browsing experience. Essential cookies keep you securely signed in as you move from page to page and remember your basic site settings. Functional and analytical cookies help us remember your match filter preferences, measure page load speeds, and understand how visitors navigate through different sections of the website.

Most web browsers accept cookies automatically, but you have the freedom to modify your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time. Please keep in mind that disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features of Find Your Partner from working properly or may require you to sign back into your account every time you refresh the page.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active and is needed to provide you with our matchmaking services. If you decide to take a break from dating, you can temporarily hide or deactivate your profile so other members can no longer view or message you while keeping your information saved for when you return.

If you choose to permanently delete your account, we will remove your public profile from the platform immediately and begin permanently erasing your personal details, photos, and messages from our active databases. Please note that we may retain minimal records for a limited period only where necessary to resolve ongoing safety investigations, prevent banned users from creating new accounts, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Personal Controls

You are always in control of your personal information on Find Your Partner. Through your account and profile settings, you have the right to access, review, edit, or update your bio, photos, and personal preferences at any time. You can also control what optional details you choose to share on your profile and manage which email notifications you receive from us.

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may also have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, object to certain types of data processing, or request the complete erasure of your records. If you ever need assistance exercising any of these rights, our support team is available to help you promptly.

9. Age Restriction and Protection of Minors

Find Your Partner is designed exclusively for adults who are 18 years of age or older. We strictly prohibit minors from registering, browsing, or creating profiles on our platform, and we do not knowingly collect or store personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that an underage person has submitted personal information or created an account, we will immediately suspend the profile and permanently delete all associated data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As Find Your Partner grows, introduces new features, and adapts to evolving privacy standards, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Whenever meaningful changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify members through an announcement on our website or via email. Continuing to use our platform after updates take effect means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.
